01 / GROUP PROJECTBUILT SYSTEM

EchoResilience

Our group built a voice-based climate alert and feedback system to help get useful information to pastoralists, farmers, and community leaders in ASAL regions, including people using basic phones.

How the system works

EchoResilience helps bridge the gap between technical climate reports and the people who need to act on them. It simplifies alerts and translates them into conversational regional dialects, then delivers them through automated phone calls. People can also call back and report local conditions such as flash floods or locust sightings in their own words. The system transcribes and translates those reports, then organizes them for a dashboard so local observations can inform wider response decisions.

We built the frontend with React and the backend with Python FastAPI. Twilio handles the voice and IVR part of the system. The language workflow uses speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and Gemini for contextual simplification and translation. The goal is to make climate information and community feedback usable without requiring a smartphone, data plan, or written literacy.

03 / FINAL YEAR PROJECTFULL STACK

KUuza

KUuza is a peer-to-peer marketplace for Kenyatta University students. The idea is to make it easier to find a buyer or seller on campus.

What it includes

Student listings, M-Pesa payments, email verification, AI-powered listing generation, and seller moderation. I worked across the full stack with React, Django REST Framework, and PostgreSQL. It is my final year project at Kenyatta University.
